What will Zillow's November 2026 home values be in 16 US cities?

The number

The Zillow Home Value Index (ZHVI) is Zillow's estimate of a typical home's value in a place, in dollars, each month.1 These markets use one version of it: single-family homes only, the middle third of values (the 33rd to 67th percentile), smoothed over three months and seasonally adjusted.1,2 Zillow's Home Values page shows all homes by default, which is a different number.3

How Zillow builds it

  • From Zestimates. Zillow estimates the value of every home, sold or not, then takes the middle of the middle-third homes in each place. Zestimate models are retrained several times a week.2
  • Smoothed. Each month is close to an average of the last three months of the unsmoothed series, so a November value rests mostly on September to November sales and listings.3
  • Published about three weeks after the month ends.2 September 2026 came out on 2026-10-15. Zillow has not yet published October; FRED, which republishes the series, lists 2026-11-19 as its next release.4 November is expected in mid-December.
  • Every release rewrites the past. When Zillow publishes a new month, it rescales a city's whole history by one factor. In one pair of releases, every month of Austin's single-family series moved up 1.70%; in another, every month of Seattle's moved down 2.54%. Across all cities the shift averages 1.6% to 1.9% either way, larger than a typical month's real change.5 The factor is unknown until the release.

The question
