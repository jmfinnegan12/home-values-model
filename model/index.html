What the research found

    How the model works
    • Center: the September value carried forward two months by the average of two pooled autoregressions on monthly changes, plus a small term for the gap between the city's and its metro's latest change. Backtested from 2023.
    • Spread: two parts. The model's own forecast error, sized per city from its recent misses. And Zillow's revision of the whole history at each release, which moves every past month by one factor; fat-tailed, standard deviation 1.55% over two releases, 10% more for Pacific Palisades and Venice.
    • The adjustment moves the center by that many percentage points. The standard deviation input stretches or shrinks the whole distribution, keeping its shape.
    • Only the first print of November counts. Kalshi's "above" is strictly greater.
